Questions

The basics before you book.

Short answers to the questions most people ask before their first visit.

How do I schedule my first appointment?

Easy! Just call Your Healing Seeds directly at 210-865-1276. Mandy's staff loves chatting with new patients to schedule you and answer any questions you have.

Do I need to prepare for my appointment?

Nope! Just come as you are. Wear comfortable clothes and bring any questions you have.

How much time should I plan for?

First visits usually take a bit longer as intial information needs to be gathered. Plan for about 15 extra minutes than listed in services section on your first visit for paperwork and questions.

What should I bring?

Just yourself and any medications you’re currently taking. Mandy likes to know the full picture of what’s happening with your health.

Will my insurance cover this?

Insurance is not accepted, although CareCredit, HSA, FSA, credit cards, and cash are accepted.

Can I text you with questions?

Yes! All patients get access to Mandy’s patient portal. If you have questions between visits, just reach out - she’s here to support your healing.

Medical oversight

Dolores Major, MD

After serving as a Captain for the U.S. Air Force Nurse Corps, Dolores Major, MD continued her education and received her Doctor of Medicine. She then completed an internship in pediatrics and a residency in anesthesiology. Dr. Major served as Department Chairman for Anesthesiology at Saint Anthony's Central Hospital in Denver, before relocating to Texas. Currently, she is involved in various committees at GRMC, including Physician Recruitment, Credentialing, and Surgical and Obstetrical services. Since 2006, Dr. Major has also been a member of the GRMC Governing Board and is currently the Vice Chairman. As a hospital-employed anesthesiologist based locally, Dr. Major is available for collaboration and is supportive of holistic approaches for the community.

Wellness services are not emergency care. Individual results vary, and all medical or prescription decisions should be made during a consultation with a qualified provider.
