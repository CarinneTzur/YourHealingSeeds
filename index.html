Questions

The basics before you book.

Short answers to the questions most people ask before their first visit.

How do I schedule?

Call Mandy directly at 210-865-1276. She will help you decide where to start.

Do ear seeds hurt?

Most clients say they barely feel them. You may notice gentle pressure once they are placed.

How long do ear seeds stay on?

Usually 3-5 days. They are designed to fall off naturally.

Do you take insurance?

The practice does not accept insurance. CareCredit, HSA, FSA, credit cards, and cash are accepted.

Do you treat children?

Ear seeds are available for ages 5 and up. Acudetox is available for ages 10 and up.
