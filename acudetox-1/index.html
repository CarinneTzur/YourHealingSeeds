What Is Acudetox?

Acudetox is a standardized 5-point ear acupuncture treatment that uses tiny sterile needles placed on specific ear points while you rest comfortably. It is often chosen for stress support, cravings, emotional balance, and overall calm.

This NADA protocol has been used in disaster relief efforts and community stress-reduction settings worldwide.

What to Expect

You will sit comfortably while the needles remain in place for about 30 to 45 minutes. Most clients feel little to no discomfort and often leave feeling deeply relaxed, lighter, and more centered.

  • Comfort-first visit with clear expectations
  • Personalized recommendation based on your goals
  • Simple aftercare or next-step guidance
